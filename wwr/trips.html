Featured Trips

Class I–II

Family Float

Half Day  |  All Ages  |  From $49/person

The perfect introduction to river rafting. This gentle float along Colorado's most scenic waterways is designed for families, beginners, and anyone who wants to enjoy the outdoors without the adrenaline. Your guide will point out local wildlife, rock formations, and tell the stories of the river.

  • Life jackets and paddles provided
  • Certified family-friendly guide
  • Riverside picnic stop included
  • Minimum age: 5 years

Class III

Canyon Rush

Full Day  |  Ages 12+  |  From $89/person

Navigate through dramatic canyon walls and energetic Class III rapids on this all-day river adventure. Canyon Rush is our most popular trip — offering just the right balance of excitement and accessibility for groups, couples, and solo travelers alike.

  • Full safety equipment included
  • Lunch on the riverbank
  • Pre-trip safety briefing
  • Group photos included

Class IV–V

Thunder Rapids Expedition

2 Days  |  Ages 16+  |  From $199/person

This is the ultimate challenge — a two-day expedition through the most demanding white water in Colorado. Only for experienced rafters or those willing to push their absolute limits. You will camp riverside overnight and hit the most powerful rapids on day two with fresh arms and fire in your eyes.

  • Overnight riverside camping
  • All meals provided
  • Expert lead and safety guide
  • Prior rafting experience recommended
